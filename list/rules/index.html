ClutterGDPS Demonlist List Leaderboard Rules

Submission Rules

To submit a record to the list, you must make sure it doesn't break any of the following rules.

  1. You MUST provide a video for your record. Pretty self-explanatory but you can't verify a record without one. We recommend using OBS to record your run.

  2. The video MUST be uploaded somewhere publicly available. Examples of publicly available video platforms include (but are not limited to) YouTube, Vimeo, or a file hosting service with no expiry time such as Catbox or 2share.

  3. You MUST provide some sort of proof within said video. Acceptable forms of proof include:
    • Audible Clicks/Source Audio.
      Either make sure your clicks are audible in your video proof, or provide a source audio (i.e. an audio file without any Geometry Dash music/sfx) in which they are.
    • BlueStacks Visual Feedback.
      BlueStacks has an option to provide a visual indicator whenever you click. To enable it, go to BlueStacks' settings, click on the "Advanced" tab, and enable "Show visual feedback for taps". If you wish to provide this kind of proof, visual feedback must be on for the entire attempt.
    • Raw Footage.
      If the video you have provided for your record is edited in any capacity, you should provide your raw footage. Raw footage is the footage directly captured by your recording software. IT MUST NOT BE EDITED WHATSOEVER.
    • Handcam.
      A video showing your hand and keyboard/mouse clicking while playing the level. You can provide this as an additional video or overlaid ontop of the gameplay, as long as it does not reasonably obscure the gameplay.

  4. If your record gets accepted, you MUST NOT delete your video proof. You are allowed to unlist the video (if on YouTube) but do not make it private or delete it.

  5. You must NOT make use of any external or internal means to make the level easier. This includes:
    • Making use of any keybinds (other than Up Arrow or Space) to click faster/more precisely/anything else. An example of this is quickly pressing shift to double click.
    • Making use of any macro software or custom macro keybinds to play parts of the level or the whole level.
    • Making use of any other Android apps inside of your emulator to make the game easier.
    • Making use of any other means to make the level less difficult to complete.

  6. You MUST NOT submit a record for anyone else. You must only submit a record for yourself. Alt accounts are not allowed.

  7. You MUST NOT exploit any loopholes in these rules. Use your common sense. If you aren't sure whether something is allowed or not, it probably isn't.
